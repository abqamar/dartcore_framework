## Known limitations

* SQLite driver is synchronous (blocks the isolate during a query).
* AOT: `dart compile exe` is rejected for programs using `sqlite3` (build hooks); use `dart build` or a non-SQLite database.
* MySQL DDL is not transactional; MariaDB untested.
* Request bodies are buffered in memory; multipart uploads are limited to 10 MiB by default.
* Handler timeouts return 504 but do not cancel the running work.
* Rate limiter and in-memory auth stores are per-process.
* `unique`/`exists` validation rules are async-only (`Validate.requestAsync`).
* Generated `make:controller --resource` does not know your columns; edit its rules (a not-null violation surfaces as a 500).
* Pre-1.0: APIs may change.
