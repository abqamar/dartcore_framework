## 0.1.0

- `dartcore create`: project scaffolding for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite or Supabase (plain Postgres), with simple, mvc, clean or modular layouts and optional JWT auth.
- `serve`, `route:list` and the migration commands (`migrate`, `migrate:rollback`, `migrate:reset`, `migrate:fresh`, `migrate:status`, `db:seed`).
- Generators: `make:controller`, `make:model`, `make:service`, `make:middleware`, `make:resource`, `make:validator`, `make:migration`, `make:seeder`.
- New: `build`, `deploy:init` and `doctor` commands for building, deployment setup and diagnosing your environment.
- Safety: validated names, no overwriting of existing files, confirmation for destructive actions.

First release.
